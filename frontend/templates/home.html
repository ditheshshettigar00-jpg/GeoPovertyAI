Research/demonstration prototype. This system estimates deprivation for a geographic area (district), learned against survey-based ground truth. It does not identify or classify individual people, and satellite/geospatial signals are treated as statistically associated with — not a direct measurement or causal explanation of — socioeconomic conditions. It is not validated for operational welfare-targeting decisions.
